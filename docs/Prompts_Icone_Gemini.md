# Prompts Gemini — ícone do app (v2)

Regras para todas: quadrado 1024×1024, **sem cantos arredondados** (o iOS arredonda), **sem texto**, sem logos, fundo opaco até a borda, elemento principal ocupando ~70% e centralizado. Anexar `Design/Arte/HeroSamurai_v3_retrato_1536x2752_original.jpeg` como referência de estilo.

## A — Busto do samurai (recomendado)
```
App icon, square 1024x1024, full-bleed background, no rounded corners, no text, no letters, no watermark.
Subject: head and shoulders of the SAME samurai from the attached reference (topknot, short beard, focused eyes, dark lamellar armor collar), three-quarter view looking right, calm and determined.
Behind his head: a large solid amber-orange sun disc (#E0702A to #C2541E), the head overlapping the disc.
Background: very dark charcoal (#121417), flat.
Style: same bold ink-outline graphic-novel style as the reference, but SIMPLIFIED for a small icon: thick clean outlines, few details, strong contrast, no tiny textures, no background scenery.
The face and topknot must stay readable at 60 px.
```

## B — Ensō + 動 (pincel)
```
App icon, square 1024x1024, full-bleed, no rounded corners, no watermark.
A single Japanese brush-calligraphy ensō circle in amber-orange (#E0702A), dry brush texture, open at the upper right.
Inside the circle, the single kanji 動 written in bold black sumi-ink brush strokes, slightly dynamic, as if in motion.
Background: warm off-white rice paper (#F2EDE4), subtle texture.
Minimal, elegant, high contrast, readable at 60 px. No other text or symbols.
```

## C — Silhueta de samurai correndo (refeita)
```
App icon, square 1024x1024, full-bleed, no rounded corners, no text, no watermark.
Bold flat silhouette of a samurai running in modern running shoes, side view, mid-stride, katana on the back, small topknot. Thick limbs, simple geometric shapes, no thin lines.
Silhouette in near-black (#141518) entirely INSIDE a large amber-orange sun disc (#E0702A); nothing crosses the circle edge.
Background: dark charcoal (#121417). Shoes off-white with one orange stripe. Flat vector style, no gradients except a subtle one on the sun.
Readable at 60 px.
```
